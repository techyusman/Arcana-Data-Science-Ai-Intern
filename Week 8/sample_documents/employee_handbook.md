# Employee Handbook

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 paid annual leave days per calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted to the employee's manager at least seven days before the planned absence. Unused leave can be carried into the next year, up to a maximum of five days.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per calendar year. A medical certificate is required when an absence continues for three or more consecutive working days.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely for up to two days per week with manager approval. Employees must remain available during the team's agreed core working hours.

## Information Security

Company information must only be stored on approved devices and services. Suspected security incidents must be reported to the IT security team immediately.
