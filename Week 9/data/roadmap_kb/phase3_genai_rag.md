# Phase 3 — Generative AI & RAG (Weeks 7–9)

## Week 7 — LLMs & Generative AI Fundamentals
Topics: LLM basics, Transformers & attention mechanism, Tokenization, Prompt engineering types, API usage (Groq / OpenRouter), Few-shot & zero-shot prompting.
Deliverable: Understanding of these concepts.

## Week 8 — Embeddings, Vector Search & RAG Pipeline
Topics: Text embeddings & similarity, Vector databases (FAISS / Chroma), Semantic search, Chunking strategies, Retrieval basics.
Deliverable: RAG pipeline architecture.

## Week 9 — End-to-End RAG Application
Topics: LangChain / LlamaIndex, Retrieval tuning & reranking, Hallucination & grounding, Building a Streamlit front-end, Evaluation of RAG outputs.
Deliverable: Working RAG chatbot with custom knowledge base.
